Strategy assumptions in V1: swing points use two candles on each side; trend requires the last two confirmed swing highs and lows to rise/fall on both H1 and H4; support/resistance uses recent confirmed swing levels; breakout must close beyond a level; retest candle must touch the level and reject it; next candle must close beyond the rejection candle's high/low. Stop is beyond the retest candle and target is 2R. These are explicit starter assumptions—not guaranteed to match your discretionary chart reading. No signal is a recommendation to trade.
